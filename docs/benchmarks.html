Protocol

Every example sees the full candidate label set for that dataset. Label wording is frozen to the publishers’ category strings. Calibration / threshold selection never touches the official test set. Two columns: current checkpoint (OneSystem v0.3.0 as published) and domain-adapted (fine-tuned on that dataset’s train split). How to train your own: see Domain-specific model in the cookbook.

What these numbers do not claim

  • They are not “exact performance” of OneSystem on every task — only on these datasets.
  • They do not measure LLM-calls-avoided end-to-end quality; that needs a workflow eval.
  • They are short-utterance benchmarks; the advertised 8,192-token window is a separate stress test.
  • Domain-adapted scores require fine-tuning on that dataset’s train split — see the cookbook.
